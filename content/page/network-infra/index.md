## Core Areas

**Routing Protocols**
- BGP (iBGP/eBGP, Route Reflectors, MP-BGP)
- OSPF (multi-area, redistribution)
- EVPN/VXLAN overlay fabric

**Datacenter Networking**
- Spine-Leaf topology design
- VTEP configuration, VNI management
- L2VPN and L3VPN with BGP EVPN
- Attack surface: BGP route poisoning, VXLAN frame injection

**Security & VPN**
- pfSense (packet filtering, NAT, VLANs)
- WireGuard, IPsec Site-to-Site VPN
- Network segmentation design

**Tools**
- GNS3 (virtualized lab environments)
- FRRouting (full-featured routing daemon)
- Wireshark (deep packet analysis, CCNA/CCNP level)
- Linux iproute2
